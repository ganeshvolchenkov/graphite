## ABOUT

The rules of company building are being rewritten in real time. The next generation of founders is right here in Europe and they're thinking globally from the start.

With AI shortening the path from idea to product, founders can now move faster than ever before, but what does the future hold and who will be building it?

AI Innovate is where Future Founders can come together, share ideas, build and meet the people worth building with across five cities. Amsterdam is the second stop and we can’t wait to see the ideas of Future Founders come to life.

Join the hackathon, imagine what comes next and build it. In one day!

## CHALLENGE

### Future Founders: From Open Model to Real Product

In one day, build a working AI product for a real customer using at least one approved open-weight model served through Nebius Token Factory. You should then be able to prove the edge your model and infrastructure choices give you: better quality, lower cost, faster performance, or adaptability.

Nebius Token Factory is your engine, required and central to what you build. Lovable and Tavily are there as optional accelerators. Build with a curated shortlist of open models across reasoning, coding, speed, long-context and multimodal, or bring your own.

Build something a real person would use, and show why it's only possible now.

## JUDGING

### Substance over showmanship

Winners are chosen by a panel, including a Nebius Token Factory judge, with the emphasis on substance over showmanship:

* Product and user value
* Problem and company potential
* Measurable model advantage
* Technical execution and use of Token Factory
* Demo clarity
* Responsible design

**Submission Requirements:**
To enter, each team submits a working product, a named customer and the problem it solves, the model and Token Factory architecture, evidence or a benchmark of the advantage, a short business case, and a live demo. Every entry is reviewed, the strongest teams pitch live, and winners are announced on the day.
